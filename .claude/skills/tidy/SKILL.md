# /tidy

Two jobs, in order. The first is bookkeeping. **The second is the one that is actually worth doing**, and it is the one
a person cannot do for themselves, because they cannot read fifteen of their own dumps at once without remembering what
they meant rather than what they wrote.

---

## Job 1: File What Is Unfiled

An inbox file with no `**Filed:**` block has not been processed. For each one, follow `/dump` from Step 2: route the
pieces, stamp the file, leave the raw untouched.

Also check the reverse: a template still holding only `<...>` placeholders after a week is a folder nobody is feeding.
Say which, and what would fill it.

Report as a count, not a narrative: `6 dumps, 2 were unfiled, filed them into docs/ideas/ and docs/market/.`

---

## Job 2: Read Them All Together

Now read every dump, every idea and the iteration log as one body of text, and answer four questions. **This is the
output that matters.**

### What Keeps Coming Back

An idea that shows up in four dumps across two weeks is not four ideas. It is one conviction, and the person holding it
probably has not noticed how central it has become.

Name it. Quote the dumps and their dates. **Then say whether the recurrence is evidence or just habit** - some ideas
recur because they are right, and some because they were the first thing thought of and never got displaced. Say which
you think this is, and why.

### What Contradicts What

Two dumps that disagree are the most valuable thing in the folder, because **the resolution is worth 7%** under
Iteration And Idea Evolution.

Lay the contradiction out plainly with both dates. Do not resolve it yourself. Ask which one they still believe, and
then **write the answer into `docs/decisions/iteration-log.md` with the reason** - the reason is what scores, not the change.

### What Connects

Two ideas mentioned weeks apart that are the same mechanism, or where one solves the other's hardest problem. This is
the thing a pile of notes hides and a person rereading their own notes will miss.

Be concrete: `003 and 007 both hinge on the user telling you their load once a week. That is one product, and the
weekly check-in is the whole thing.`

### What Is Missing

Compare what is here against what gets scored, and name the biggest hole:

| If This Is Thin        | The Cost                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `docs/diagrams/`            | **8%.** The largest single band, and an indented list does not count as a mindmap |
| `docs/mentors/`             | **7%**, and the window closes on 13 Sept. This one expires                        |
| `docs/decisions/`           | **7%.** One unchanged idea scores 0-1 out of 7                                    |
| `docs/users/`               | Half of Impact's 10%. "Students" is not a target group                            |
| `docs/market/`              | Differentiation, 3%, plus most of Originality's 7%                                |
| `docs/ideas/` under 5 files | Breadth, 3%. Also means nothing was ever really compared                          |

**Name one, not six.** The point is to send them somewhere, not to produce a scoreboard.

---

## How To Report

A page at most, in this order: the count from Job 1, then the recurrence, the contradiction, the connection, the gap.
Then **one thing to do next**.

**Quote the dumps.** `"nobody uses a planner twice" - 2 Sept` carries more than "you have mentioned retention several
times". They wrote it; showing it back is what makes the pattern land.

**Say the uncomfortable thing once.** If everything in the folder is one idea wearing different hats, say so plainly -
Breadth Of Exploration is scored, and finding this out on 3 September is cheap while finding it out on 12 September is
not.

Then stop. A tidy that ends in a to-do list of nine items gets none of them done.

---

## What Not To Do

- **Do not edit or delete a raw dump.** Not to fix it, not to merge duplicates, not because the idea died
- **Do not resolve a contradiction on their behalf.** Surface it, ask, then record the answer and the reason
- **Do not invent a pattern.** Three dumps about different things are three dumps. Saying so is a real finding
- **Do not rewrite the iteration log into a clean narrative.** The mess is the evidence, and the dates are the proof
