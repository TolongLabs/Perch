# Decisions

**Worth 7%** (Iteration And Idea Evolution) - the second-largest single band in the whole rubric.

> "Multiple documented iterations showing how and why the idea evolved, **including dropped directions**." — the rubric,
> 6-7/7 band
>
> "Dead ends are worth points. Document them." — the organisers, presenting the rubric

Two files, and they are the most valuable things on this branch.

| File                 | Holds                                                            |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `iteration-log.md`   | Every time our thinking changed, dated, with the reason         |
| `dropped.md`         | The graveyard. What we killed, and what killed it               |

## What "Done" Looks Like

- **More than one real turn.** One idea, unchanged start to finish, scores 0-1 out of 7. A couple of minor tweaks
  scores 2-3. The top band needs several documented turns **and** the directions we abandoned
- Every entry has a **reason**, not just a change. "Switched to X" is worth little; "switched to X because the mentor
  pointed out Y" is worth the marks
- The dates are real. A log written in one sitting on 12 September reads exactly like a log written in one sitting on
  12 September

## Do Not

- Do not tidy the log into a clean narrative. The mess is the evidence
- Do not delete a dropped idea to make us look decisive. It does the opposite
