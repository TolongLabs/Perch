Take care before and during

Things about this place that are easy to get wrong, plus a feed of what is happening there right now.

Before you go

  • Language avoid

    Do not use the word "babi" (pig) casually. In Indonesia it is a strong insult, not just an animal.

  • Water note

    Tap water is not for drinking. Bottled or boiled only, including for brushing teeth.

  • Temples note

    Shoulders and knees covered at Borobudur and Prambanan. Sarongs are lent at the gate.

  • Cash tip

    Small warungs and jeep drivers take cash only. Carry rupiah in small notes.
