## Slide 16 - Problem Statement

### Problem Statement 1 - Stress & Workload Manager

Build an app that gives students a clear picture of their load across different areas (mental, time, physical, social,
errands) and actually helps them do something about it before burnout hits. It shouldn’t just track and report. Instead,
it should help students rebalance what they’re carrying and push them toward recovery, like rest or getting out of the
house. Make sure it’s usable, accessible, and something students would actually keep open on their phone.

Track: Lifestyle & Personal Productivity

### Problem Statement 2 - Travel Planner

Build something that helps people plan a trip from start to finish with budgeting, building an itinerary, syncing
preferences across a group, and adjusting plans when things don’t go as expected. It should make planning faster and
less stressful, and work well whether someone’s travelling solo or with a group.

Track: Lifestyle & Personal Productivity

## Slide 18 - Ideation - 25%

- Visual diagrams and mindmaps (8%). A mindmap, problem tree or a user flow that clearly shows how you ideated.
- Iteration and idea evolution (7%). Multiple documented iterations showing how and why the idea changed, including the
  directions you dropped. Dead ends are worth points. Document them.
- Mentor consultation and feedback integration (7%).
- Breadth of exploration (3%).

## Slide 19 - Impact - 20%

- Understanding the problem context (5%). Show you understand the challenge, its causes, its stakeholders, and its
  real-world implications.
- Target group alignment (5%).
- Effectiveness of the solution (7%).
- Reach and scalability (3%).

## Slide 20 - Creativity - 15%

- Originality (7%). Original approach or a novel combination of existing ideas.
- Novel features or twists (5%). Multiple clever features, or one standout twist that defines the product, is better.
- Differentiation (3%). Why yours is different and better than what exists.

## Slide 21 - Feasibility - 15%

- Technical viability and tech stack (6%). Appropriate technologies with a realistic implementation approach.
- Planning and scope realism (5%). A clear, achievable scope with a concrete build plan.
- Resource and time awareness (4%). Show a realistic grasp of time, skills, and cost.

## Slide 22 - Presentation - 15%

- Clarity of explanation (5%)
- Structure and flow (4%)
- Delivery and confidence (4%)
- Engagement and persuasiveness (2%)

## Slide 23 - Design - 10%

- Visual consistency (4%)
- Usability and UX (4%)
- Mockup completeness (2%)
