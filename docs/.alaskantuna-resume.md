### A Correction Worth Remembering

**The first mentor shortlist I gave was wrong, and wrong in the dangerous direction.** It came from a text export of the
booking workbook, where a blacked-out cell and an empty cell are indistinguishable — so every mentor blackout got
counted as a free slot. Khor Jia Quan looked wide open on 8 Sept; he had blacked the whole day out.

Re-read from the actual cell fills, six mentors were at zero for the rest of the window and **both CodeNection champions
were effectively gone**. The lesson generalises: when a spreadsheet carries meaning in formatting, a text export
silently discards it.

### The Storybook Fork

The Excalidraw session produced a page flow with the interview, a loading state and the storybook — plus **Plan It
Together floating unconnected to anything**, which turned out to be the canvas telling the truth.

Two drafts were built and compared by eye:

|                | **A — One Book, Two States**      | **B — Planner, Then Book**                    |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Voting happens | On the book's own pages           | On a separate planner page                    |
| The book is    | Editable forever, via the bench   | Read-only output                              |
| A closure      | Rewrites the page you are reading | Blocks the reprint until the group re-settles |

**B was chosen.** The reasoning that settled it was aimed at B, not A: _is the planner just more interview?_ It is — a
bounded set of decisions with a progress meter is the same machine as a five-question interview. That argument says B
collapses into A. B was picked anyway, which means **the planner has to earn its separation by being a durable shared
surface the group lives in over days**, not a screen they pass through. That is now a design requirement, not an open
question.

**What B cost:** the best demonstration of the headline claim. A rewrote the page under the reader; B raises an alert
and hands back four steps. The mitigation is on skeleton screen 10 — a one-tap **"Use The Bench"** straight from the
alert that skips the planner entirely.

A is preserved whole in `research:docs/decisions/dropped.md`, not summarised, because the interaction may need to come
back and because dropped directions score.

### The Interview Was Asking The Wrong Things

The eight questions on the canvas were a booking form — start and end, transport, hotel stars, budget, dates. **Nothing
in them feeds the bench**, and after three competitor scans the bench is the only originality claim still standing.

Replaced with five questions that each produce ranking signal, plus a deliberately plain one-screen form for the boring
parts. **Question 3, "pick the three you'd hate to miss" from a grid of ten, is the whole point** — three chosen and
seven benched is a ranking, and that ranking is the bench.

Full reasoning, including the social-post front door, where the AI agent's tool calls belong, and why "Edit My Plan"
should not exist as a button: `research:docs/decisions/storybook-shape.md`.
