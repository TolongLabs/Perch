# Scan: The Five Pages Of The Slide-Deck Mockup

**Scanned 2026-09-06**, after [`../prototype/travel-planner-slides.html`](../prototype/travel-planner-slides.html) was
built. One question: **does each of those five pages already exist as a shipping product?**

The honest summary first. **Four of the five pages are occupied, two of them by mature product categories we did not
know existed.** And the scan turned up something worse: **a product that already does the round-trip differentiator**,
which was the last originality claim the 1 September scan left standing. That is recorded in
[`competitors.md`](competitors.md) and [`landscape.md`](landscape.md), and it is the most important thing on this page.

Everything below was read on 6 September 2026. Where a page refused to load and only the search summary was available,
it is marked `[snippet only]`.

---

## Page 1 - Poster And Itinerary

**Occupied, by a B2B category none of us had looked at: itinerary builders for travel agents.** Their entire output is
the artefact our page 1 draws - a branded, poster-led, day-by-day trip document handed to a client.

| Product     | What It Is                                                                                       |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Travefy** | "quickly create stunning, branded itineraries with an easy drag-and-drop builder"; describes itself as the #1 itinerary builder voted by travel agents |
| **Axus**    | Itinerary management for luxury advisors, big in the Virtuoso network. "branded itineraries so your company's personality shines through" |
| **Vamoos**  | "beautifully branded, visually stunning mobile experience" for small tour operators. Vamoos Studio builds bespoke per-trip tools |
| **mTrip**   | Same category; publishes the comparison round-ups these came from                                |

**What this means for us.** The deck format is not a novelty, it is an established genre with paying customers. That is
not fatal - none of these is aimed at four friends planning their own trip, they are aimed at an agent producing a
document for a client. But **"we made it look like a slide deck" is not a differentiator** and must not be pitched as
one.

## Page 2 - Dates, Then Places

**Occupied, and this was already half-known.** The date-strip-then-vote flow is the core of a whole group-travel
category.

| Product           | The Overlap                                                                          |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Troupe**        | Built for "where should we go and when" - polls, **ranked voting**, RSVPs. The closest thing found to our vote-and-bench mechanism |
| **Howbout**       | Group shares availability, polls to vote on activities                               |
| **WhenAvailable** | Date-option polls by shared link, no account needed                                  |
| **SquadTrip**     | Polling on destinations, activities, restaurants **and dates**                       |
| **WePlanify**     | Collaborative day-by-day itinerary, group polls, shared budget with automatic settlement, packing lists, free |

**Troupe's ranked voting is the one to check properly.** Our ranked-bench claim - that losing votes stay ranked and
become both the cut list and the replacement pool - needs testing against what Troupe actually does with its rankings.
**`[not verified]`**: Troupe was found in comparison articles, not read directly. Settle it before the bench is pitched
as novel.

## Page 3 - Take Care, And The News Feed

**Occupied twice over, and one of the occupiers is free, official, and better than ours for the exact example we used.**

**The cautions half** exists inside travel-safety apps that ship etiquette as a feature:

- **Travel Smart App** - "Do's, dont's & dangers where ever you are", "Latest travel tips & cultural advice", plus scam
  and dangerous-area warnings. Read directly; it does **not** do itineraries
- **World Travel Protection's Travel Assist** - etiquette guidance for formal settings, gift-giving in Japan named as
  an example `[snippet only]`
- **Cover-More** (Australia and New Zealand) - cultural insights, health tips, unusual local laws `[snippet only]`
- **TripLingo** - phrasebook plus culture notes
- **Wanderboat** - publishes cultural-taboo guides, but as blog content beside the AI planner, **not inside the
  itinerary**. The nearest thing to an unoccupied seam on this page

**The news half** is a mature category with real infrastructure behind it:

- **Sitata** - monitors world media 24/7 with AI plus human intelligence analysts, and alerts you "based on your exact
  itinerary or location" across 200+ countries, with "what to do" advice on a map. Flight delays, outbreaks, transit
  strikes, violence
- **GeoSure** - real-time alerts on natural disasters, political unrest, health threats `[snippet only]`

**And for Japan specifically, the government already ships this.** The **"Safety tips"** app, supervised by the **Japan
Tourism Agency**, pushes earthquake, tsunami, typhoon, volcano and heavy-rain warnings to foreign visitors in **14
languages**, with an evacuation flowchart, useful phrases, and a list of medical facilities that accept foreigners. It
is free.

**What this means for us.** Our page 3 is a hand-updated list. **Sitata is itinerary-aware and ours is not** - it knows
which of your stops the flood affects. That is the gap on our own page, and it is the same gap the map-first mockup
already closed with the bench and automatic substitution. The cautions half has a thinner seam: nothing found puts the
taboos **inside the itinerary, at the stop where they apply**.

## Page 4 - Photo Spots

**The most heavily occupied page of the five. This is not a feature, it is an entire product category**, and we walked
into the middle of it.

| Product                     | What It Does                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Locationscout**           | **233,000+ photo spots across 184+ countries**, submitted by photographers who shot them |
| **PhotoHound**              | Per spot: exact GPS, what3words, **what to shoot**, sample images, gear suggestions, weather, sunrise and sunset. Premium adds hourly 7-day forecast, rain radar, cloud cover. Curated by professional photographers. **And it builds itineraries from spots.** Lists 85 Japan spots `[snippet only]` |
| **LightScout**              | AI-generated photo guides per location: **golden-hour timing, weather context, composition tips, directions**, tuned to your camera and skill level |
| **LightPlan**               | 3D sun and moon, 3D terrain, cloud overlays; saves locations **with custom camera angles** |
| **PhotoPills**, **TPE**     | Where the light will fall at a given spot on a given date, before you book            |
| **Voyay**                   | An AI walking-tour app that markets itself as "your personal photo scout... the right doorways and rooftops at the right time" |
| **Wanderlog**, **Mindtrip** | Both already surface photo spots inside a general planner. Mindtrip publishes Japan photo-spot maps |

**What this means for us.** Every element of our page 4 - the angle, the time window, the thing to avoid - is shipped,
with real data, by several products. **PhotoHound and LightScout beat our page outright**, and PhotoHound also does the
itinerary. Our version has no data source at all. Pitching photo angles as a novel feature in front of judges is the
single most likely way to lose the Originality band on this idea.

## Page 5 - The Google Maps Handoff

**Not novel, and it does not need to be.** Wanderlog already pins every added place on a Google Maps-based map and
exports places to Google Maps. This page is a **feasibility decision, not an originality claim** - it removes the
metered map API that the idea log named as the honest deployment risk. Keep it; pitch it under Feasibility, never under
Creativity.

---

## What This Scan Changes

1. **Page 4 should not be pitched as a feature.** It is a category with better-resourced incumbents and we have no data.
2. **Page 3 has a real seam, but it is not the one we built:** cautions and disruptions **attached to the stop they
   affect**, changing the itinerary. Sitata already does the itinerary-awareness; nothing found does the
   taboo-at-the-stop.
3. **Page 1 is a genre, not an invention.**
4. **The round-trip claim is damaged.** See [`competitors.md`](competitors.md) - Contour and calimoto.
5. **Troupe needs reading directly** before the ranked bench is claimed as ours.
