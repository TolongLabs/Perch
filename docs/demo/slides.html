The Moment

The Day Says What It Did

She drags a stop out of its cluster on purpose. Day 1 turns gold, and the reason is written beside it.

Fits

Every stop fits its opening hours, and the day’s travel plus dwell fits 09:00 to 21:00.

Fits But Runs Slow

It still fits, but the order runs more than 25 percent slower than the scheduler’s own.

Overruns

A stop is outside its opening hours, or the day runs past 21:00.

Cluster by area, order by best period and opening hours, nearest neighbour within the day. One describable rule, and the colour is the explanation.

Not broken. Slow. The instrument answers back the moment she overrules it, and the call stays hers.
