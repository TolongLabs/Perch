# The UN Global Goals icons

`sdg-08.png`, `sdg-11.png`, `sdg-12.png` are the United Nations' own SDG icons, downloaded
from the UN's Sustainable Development Goals site on 11 September 2026:

    https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/goals/E_SDG_Icons-08.jpg
    https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/goals/E_SDG_Icons-11.jpg
    https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/goals/E_SDG_Icons-12.jpg

**Resized from 466px to 400px and converted to PNG. Nothing else was changed** - no recolour,
no crop, no rounded corners, no added type. The UN's guidelines for the communications
material ask that the icons are not altered, and each icon already carries its own numeral
and goal title, which is why the deck gives them no frame and no caption of its own.

The icons are used on slides 01 and 18 to state which goals Perch aligns with. They are not
a claim of UN endorsement.
