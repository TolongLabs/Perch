# Users

Feeds **Impact, 20%** - specifically Understanding The Problem Context (5%) and Target Group Alignment (5%).

> "Name a specific user, not just all students, and then show the before and after, life without your app and life with
> it. If you can't say that in a sentence, you should fix that first." — the organisers, presenting the rubric
>
> A broad "everyone" audience scores **2-3 out of 5.**

## What Goes Here

| File               | Holds                                                                      |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `personas.md`      | Who exactly we are building for, and who we are deliberately not            |
| `interviews/`      | One file per real conversation with a real person. Copy `_template.md`      |

## What "Done" Looks Like

- **At least three real conversations** with people in the target group. Three is not a research programme, but it is
  three more than most teams do, and it is the difference between a quote and a guess
- One named, specific primary user in `personas.md`, with the before-and-after written in a single sentence
- **A written note of who we are not building for.** Excluding someone on purpose is what makes a target group a target
  group

## Do Not

- Do not write a persona from imagination and then go looking for people who match it
- Do not quote someone who does not exist. If it is a guess, mark it `[assumed]`
