> i am looking to build a travel planner that would looks like google map, where there is a map
> on the right and inserts on the left, so first we would choose our destination country and
> airports to touch down and fly back, next there would be budget as well as travelling mode
> (in person/group, self driving, public transport), so when the touch down and end point is
> picked, find the best pathway to link the start and end and find best travel recommended
> location in between. what if they would start and end in the same airport? in this case there
> will be 2 option, either we travel for 90% of the days and the last day we would go the exact
> way but back to airport, or we spend 50% of our time playing to the furthest way from the
> airport and play our way back

Later additions: trip length in days, accommodation from youth hostel to 5-star, and *discretion* —
extra days and extra budget a solo traveller would stretch to.
